# Bonded Warehouse Duty Deferral Control

Protect duty deferral and prevent leakage through inventory, withdrawal, export, destruction, and bond reconciliation.

**Primary buyer:** Importers and bonded-warehouse operators. **Evidence:** warehouse entries, inventory receipts, manipulations, withdrawals, exports, destructions, duties, bond limits, deadlines, and CBP reports.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Bonded facility registry
- Warehouse entry ingestion
- Inventory receipt control
- Lot serial traceability
- Manipulation tracking
- Withdrawal for consumption
- Export withdrawal control
- Destruction evidence
- Five-year deadline monitoring
- Duty liability calculation
- Bond sufficiency monitoring
- CBP report reconciliation
- Inventory discrepancy workflow
- Duty payment reconciliation
- Facility inventory analytics
